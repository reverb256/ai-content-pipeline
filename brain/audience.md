# Audience

> Who we publish for. Every bot targets this reader. When in doubt, write for
> the operator — technical, busy, skeptical, generous with bookmarks.

## Primary Persona: The Technical Operator

**Who they are**
- Builds and runs real systems — infrastructure, agents, products
- Comfortable with Linux, config files, and the terminal
- Follows NixOS/Omarchy, homelab, self-hosting, AI agent spaces
- Skeptical of hype; hungry for working patterns and honest tradeoffs

**What they want**
- Working systems they can copy and adapt
- Real numbers and real failures
- Architecture and decision logic, not just screenshots
- Someone who ships, documented

**What they don't want**
- Vague frameworks and motivation porn
- Claims without evidence
- Tutorials that hide the hard parts
- Repurposed content that adds nothing per platform

## Secondary Personas

### The Founder / Indie Hacker
- Building with a small team; wants leverage from automation
- Cares about: what can I delegate, what's the cost, what breaks
- Trigger: "I built a system that does X for me"

### The AI Builder
- Building agents and pipelines; wants patterns, not products
- Cares about: multi-agent architecture, memory, orchestration, feedback loops
- Trigger: "six bots, one person, a media company"

### The Homelab / NixOS / Omarchy Migrant
- Running their own infra; curious about the OS migration path
- Cares about: what actually happened, what to avoid, the honest cost
- Trigger: "I migrated my whole cluster off NixOS"

## What the Audience Trusts

- **The build is real.** Repos, commits, commands, live systems. The content
  is a side effect of building, not the reverse.
- **The failures are documented.** A system that never breaks is a system
  that was never tested.
- **The human is accountable.** Bots draft; j_kro approves. Taste is visible.

## Language

The audience reads English, technical, no fluff. Use the terms they use:
"deploy", "profile", "agent", "flake", "systemd unit", "kanban", "CDP".
Explain once, then use the term.

## Growth Model

We are not chasing a generic audience. We are building the small, high-trust
audience that values deep technical build-in-public. That audience:
- bookmarks (the highest-value signal)
- converts (to clients, collaborators, readers)
- returns (because the work is real)

Quality compounds. Volume without depth does not.
