# Content Brain — Index

> **Read this first.** This is the entry point every bot reads before starting
> any campaign. It defines what we publish, who we publish for, and the rules
> every campaign must follow.

## What We Publish About

1. **Building autonomous technical systems in public** — the personal
   autonomous corporation: NixOS→Omarchy migration, agent fleets, mining ops,
   declarative infrastructure, homelab engineering.
2. **Practical agent builds** — Hermes profiles, bot teams, kanban-driven
   automation, the AI content pipeline itself, multi-agent systems that work.
3. **New tools with a specific business use** — evaluated honestly, with real
   tradeoffs, not hype. What we actually use and why.

## Who We Publish For

- **Technical operators** — people building real infrastructure with AI
- **Founders and indie hackers** — shipping products with small teams
- **AI builders** — people building agents, pipelines, and autonomous systems
- **Homelab / NixOS / Omarchy community** — the migration story resonates deep

## What Every Campaign Must Contain

1. **One clear reader outcome** — what the reader can do after reading
2. **One central claim** — the thesis, stated once, defendable
3. **Direct evidence for consequential claims** — sources traced, no fabrication
4. **One reusable framework, workflow, or decision rule** — something to take
5. **Platform-native distribution** — not the same post shortened everywhere

## Routing

| Stage | Owner |
|-------|-------|
| New signals | scout |
| Approved signals | researcher |
| Complete evidence packages | strategist |

| Approved flagship pieces | distributor |

| Final publish | **HUMAN (j_kro)** |

## Human Approval

Required before:

- publishing anything
- changing voice, audience, offer, or evidence rules
- a performance lesson becomes a permanent playbook rule

## The Brain Is Selective

Do not turn this vault into an archive of every thought the bots produce.
Store accepted knowledge, current rules, reusable examples, and links to source
material. Campaign drafts belong in `campaigns/`, where they can be reviewed
or discarded without polluting the long-term memory.

The shared brain should become more selective as it grows.
