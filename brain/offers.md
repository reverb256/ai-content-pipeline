# Offers

> What the audience can take from us, and (eventually) what we sell. This is
> the value layer — every campaign should give the reader a reason to keep
> coming back.

## Current Offers (2026-08)

There are no paid offers yet. The current offers are the content itself:

1. **The working system.** Every campaign documents a real build with real
   code, real commands, and real tradeoffs. Readers can copy and adapt.
2. **The honest post-mortem.** What broke, what it cost, what we'd do
   differently. Rare and valuable.
3. **The build-in-public story.** Following a real autonomous corporation
   being built, week by week, with real numbers.

## Offer Principles

1. **Give the framework free; charge for the implementation.** The insight is
   free. The working, adapted-to-you version is the product.
2. **Proof before pitch.** The audience converts because the build is real and
   the numbers are visible, not because we ask.
3. **One offer at a time.** When an offer exists, it is singular and clear.
   The catalog does not sprawl.

## Campaign Tie-In

Every campaign's distribution should leave the reader with a reason to follow:
- the next build in the series
- the playbook or template they can take
- the question the campaign raised that the next one answers

The offer is the loop continuing, not a CTA button.
