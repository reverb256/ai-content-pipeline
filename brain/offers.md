## Revenue Rules (HARD)

1. **Every campaign names its offer.** `campaign.md` has an Offers section.
   If a campaign has no revenue answer, the strategist says so before the
   writer starts.
2. **The CTA matches the reader's stage.** Aware → follow/subscribe.
   Interested → flagship/list. Considering → the offer. Ready → buy.
   A "buy now" on an aware-stage post is revenue-negative.
3. **Track conversion, not just engagement.** 100k views with 0 conversions
   is a distribution win and a revenue miss. Every clip records its
   conversion path.
4. **Proof before pitch.** The audience converts because the build is real
   (261 TH/s, 391GB backups intact, 0 failed units), not because we ask.
5. **One offer at a time.** When an offer exists, it is singular and clear.
   The catalog does not sprawl.
6. **Free content = the "what". Paid content = the "how".** The insight is
   free; the working, adapted-to-you implementation is the product.
7. **Affiliates are tagged and tracked.** Tools we genuinely use and
   recommend (Omarchy-adjacent, mining hardware, AI tools) are affiliate
   candidates — but only if we use them and they fit the audience.
