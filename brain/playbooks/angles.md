# Angles Playbook

> One complete angle is more useful than ten interchangeable ideas. The
> strategist returns ONE recommendation and explains why the rejected
> directions are weaker.

## The Angle Brief Contract

```
reader:
reader outcome:
current source:
central tension:
thesis:
what becomes possible:
flagship format:
reusable object:
proof required:
sections:
distribution entryways:
```

## What Makes an Angle Strong

1. **A specific reader.** Not "developers" — "the operator running 4 hosts
   who is tired of NixOS rebuilds."
2. **A real tension.** The angle lives in the conflict: "faster writing does
   not solve weak ideas, duplicated distribution, or lost learning."
3. **A thesis that can be defended.** One central claim, stated once, with
   evidence.
4. **A reusable object.** Framework, workflow, decision rule — the reader
   leaves with something they can take.
5. **Platform entryways.** The same idea has 5 different doors in: proof,
   mechanism, workflow, risk, result.

## Our Angle Library (build-in-public pillars)

| Pillar | Central tension | Reusable object |
|--------|----------------|-----------------|
| The Migration | Declarative purity vs. working systems | The migration decision framework |
| Agent Fleet | More bots ≠ more output | The handoff contract pattern |

| Omnigate | Migration vs. lock-in | The skip-ladder principle |

## Angle Anti-Patterns

- "Here's a thing I built" without a reader outcome
- Ten interchangeable ideas instead of one complete one
- Picking the headline first, then finding evidence to support it
- Angles that repeat what the audience has already seen 10x
- No reusable object — the piece ends and the reader has nothing to take

## How to Reject Angles

The strategist explains WHY each rejected direction is weaker:
- "X repeats the last three posts on this topic"
- "X requires proof we don't have"
- "X serves the founder persona but not the operator"
- "X has no reusable object — it is a story, not a takeaway"

Rejection logic is part of the angle brief. It teaches the rest of the team.
