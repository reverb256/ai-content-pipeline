# Platforms Playbook

> Platform-native distribution. Repurposing fails when the system treats
> formatting as distribution. Each platform gets the part of the idea that
> fits its consumption pattern.

## Platform Roles

| Platform | Role | What it gets |
|----------|------|--------------|
| **X** | Discovery + real-time | Sharpest claim, proof point, build sequence, thread |
| **LinkedIn** | Professional/B2B | Operator lesson, internal decision, before/after workflow |
| **Substack** | Owned audience | The nuance, examples, personal context that would overload a post |
| **YouTube** | Authority + visual | Spoken narrative around tension, demonstration, result |
| **Blog** | Permanent library | The full deep-dive, SEO, the archive that compounds |

## The Distribution Rule

Every asset must give someone a reason to consume it even if they already saw
another part of the campaign. Five hooks making the same claim = failure.

## X (Primary)

- 3-5 posts/day sustained, spaced 3+ hours
- 1 thread/week (the flagship format)
- Links in the first reply, never in the main post
- 0-2 hashtags
- Replies are the growth engine: 10-20 thoughtful replies/day to accounts
  10-100x our size
- Post when audience is active; reply to every reply in the first hour

## Substack

- 1 high-quality issue/week + 2-4 Notes/day
- Free content = the "what". Paid content = the "how" (not active yet)
- Notes are the discovery engine (>35% of subs come from Notes for many
  creators)
- Recommendations network: recommend + ask for reciprocation
- SEO matters: keyword-rich titles, meta, internal links

## YouTube

- 2-4 long-form/week max (diminishing returns beyond); Shorts for discovery
- First 30 seconds decide everything — hook within the first 2 seconds of
  Shorts
- Thumbnails + titles earn the click; retention keeps it
- Niche clarity: one topic, tight cluster
- Playlists stretch sessions; cards/end-screens are retention nudges, not
  growth engines
- Auto-dubbing = global reach by default

## Blog

- Deep technical essays: system design, post-mortems, decision essays
- 1-2 posts/week
- SEO + permanent archive; every post compounds
- The blog is the portfolio — it demonstrates depth AND communication

## Platform Anti-Patterns

- Same post shortened 7 times (the cardinal sin)
- A carousel that just quotes the flagship piece
- CTAs that don't match the reader's stage
- Tone drift between platforms
- Posting code without context
- Broadening appeal by diluting technical depth (the depth IS the moat)

## Cross-Platform Discipline

- Start from the angle brief, not the finished article
- Ask: what part of the idea fits THIS platform's consumption pattern?
- Measure per-platform; double down on winners, drop flat formats
- Repurpose winning formats with fresh angles, not old content re-posted
