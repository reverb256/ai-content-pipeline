## BODY

### Section 1: The Numbers That Do Not Add Up (1:30–3:00)

Let me give you the full trajectory.

2019: $3 million ARR.
2021: $31 million ARR.
2022: $67 million ARR.
2023: $250 million ARR.
2024: $400 million ARR.
2025: $600 million ARR.
July 2026: an estimated $865 million ARR.

Source: SaaStr and Sacra.
Notion is private. These are estimates.
But the direction is not in dispute.

Now the valuation.

2021: Series C at $10 billion.
January 2026: $270 million employee tender offer at $11 billion.

Five years. One billion dollars of valuation increase.
Meanwhile, revenue grew by roughly 200 times.

The multiple went from 322x to 18x.
Investors now price Notion like a public company.

This is the paradox.
This is the lesson.

### Section 2: How They Survived the Compression (3:00–4:30)

Most companies fail here.
Their growth slows right when the market demands profitability.
The multiple compresses and the company cannot fundraise.

Notion avoided this for one reason.

It raised only $343 million total.
No VC board seats.
Ivan Zhao owns roughly 30%.

Source: SaaStr, Contrary Research, Forbes.

Profitability before leverage gave Zhao something rare.
He could say no.

Sequoia and Index competed hard to get in.
The result: no VCs on the board.
Extreme founder control.

When the market turned in 2022, Notion did not need to raise.
It was already efficient.
It kept growing on its own revenue.

The lesson: capital efficiency is not about raising less.
It is about keeping the right to say no.

### Section 3: The Bottoms-Up Motion (4:30–6:00)

Notion did not sell to enterprises first.
It sold to individuals.
Then those individuals pulled it into organizations.

This is B2C2B.
Product-led growth at the bottom.
Enterprise sales on top.

Source: MarkHub24, Decibel VC.

The mechanism is simple.
One person starts using Notion for personal notes.
They bring it to their team.
The team brings it to their department.
The department brings it to IT.

Sales engages only after users are already embedded.
By the time a procurement conversation happens, the product has already won.

This is why Notion passed 100 million users in August 2024.
Source: Axis Intelligence, HTX.

Not 100 million paying users.
100 million users.
The top of the funnel is enormous.

### Section 4: The Template Economy as SEO Moat (6:00–7:15)

Here is something most people miss.

Notion has over 10,000 user-generated templates.
Project roadmaps. CRM boards. Habit trackers. Meeting notes.

Each template is a long-tail search asset.
"Project roadmap template" — that is a search query.
"Freelance CRM template" — another query.

Users create the marketing content.
Notion does not pay for it.
It ranks for it.

This is an SEO moat that competitors cannot copy.
Airtable does not have this.
Coda does not have this.
Notion's community built it for free.

The ambassador program amplifies this.
300-plus ambassadors across 30-plus countries.
No monetary compensation.
They write books. They run YouTube channels. They organize meetups.

Source: Decibel VC.

Notion did not pay for this distribution.
The community wanted to be part of something.

### Section 5: The AI Bundling Trick (7:15–8:45)

This is the move that changed everything.

In 2024, AI was an add-on.
Eight to ten dollars per user.
Attach rate: 10 to 20%.

In May 2025, Notion bundled AI exclusively into Business and Enterprise tiers.
Twenty dollars per user per month.

Source: MegaOneAI, CNBC.

This forced a whole-team decision.
Either the whole team gets AI.
Or nobody does.

The attach rate doubled in twelve months.
From roughly 20% to 50%-plus.

By the end of 2025, more than half of Notion's ARR came from AI-enabled customers.

Then came May 2026.
Custom Agents.
Shareable, trigger-driven agents that inherit the creator's permissions.

One million agents built in the first two months.
Source: Sacra, The Next Web.

Notion is no longer selling seats.
It is selling work.

Custom Agents are priced via consumption credits.
Ten dollars per 1,000 credits.
First non-seat revenue line.

Source: COO Akshay Kothari.

**Visual note:** Timeline graphic. 2024: "AI add-on, 20% attach." 2025: "Bundled in Business tier." 2026: "1M agents built." Arrow showing attach rate climbing from 20% to 50%+.

### Section 6: The Notion Mail Shutdown (8:45–10:00)

On September 22, 2026, Notion Mail shuts down.

Source: The Verge, TechCrunch.

An 18-month-old product.
Killed.

Why?
Because more than 50% of Notion Mail users already managed email without opening the inbox.
The agents handled it.

Maintaining a standalone email client became a distraction from the agent platform.

This is the clearest possible signal of strategic direction.
Killing a product is rare.
Killing it because agents made it obsolete is a statement.

The inbox is dying.
Notion is betting everything on agents.
