## Signal

- **event:** zephyr + nexus migrated off NixOS to Omarchy 4.0.0 (2026-08). The
  personal autonomous corporation runs on Omarchy with a Hermes agent fleet,
  memlawb memory, kanban production desk, and a headless CDP browser driving
  real platform sessions.
- **source:** omarchy-fleet-migration-direction (2026-08-24, locked by j_kro);
  infra/nexus-is-omarchy-now (verified live 2026-08-26);
  ~/Projects/Reverb-OS (migration home, github.com/reverb256/Reverb-OS);
  omnigate (reverb256/omnigate, the migration tool)
- **urgency:** HIGH — the migration story is novel (few people have moved a
  whole cluster off NixOS), the migration direction is locked, and the proof
  is fresh and verifiable. Decay: moderate — the "we just did this" window
  lasts weeks, the "here is the playbook" value lasts months.
- **audience question:** How do you migrate a NixOS cluster to Omarchy without
  losing your data, your services, or your sanity — and what does the
  autonomous-corporation setup look like on the other side?
- **authority clip / proof object:** nexus migration outcome (0 failed units vs
  sentry's 8, forge's 2 on NixOS); 77 sops secrets decrypt via YubiKey only;
  garage 391GB backups intact through emergency wipe; memlawb encrypted memory
