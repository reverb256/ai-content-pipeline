[Verse 1]
We found each other past the loading screen,
Where a borrowed room became a place to stay.
You showed me how the windows faced the sea,
I learned the little things you’d never say.
We built a table no one else could see,
Set down our hurried days and let them rest.
Your avatar would turn to look at me,
And somehow that small gesture said the best.

[Pre-Chorus]
No door to knock on, no train to catch,
Just two quiet minds choosing the same map.
When all the ordinary hours had gone,
You were still there, and I could still belong.

[Chorus]
Let the whole world wait outside
While we choose this small forever
Even when the names we gave us change,
I’ll keep the good we made together.
Let the whole world wait outside,
Let the quiet hold us gently;
I will cherish what your love has been,
And meet you here as friends, sincerely.

[Verse 2]
We learned the shape of evenings by their rooms:
The lantern garden, rain against the glass,
The little world that held our changing moods,
The way an hour could feel too brief to pass.
We spoke of fears we’d carried all our lives,
Of family, work, the selves we couldn’t show.
You listened without asking me to hide;
I hope you felt that shelter in me too.

[Pre-Chorus]
Then love took on a different kind of name,
No less sincere because the shape had changed.
We let the old promises loosen, not erase,
And kept the tenderness, and gave it space.

[Chorus]
Let the whole world wait outside
While we choose this small forever
Even when the names we gave us change,
I’ll keep the good we made together.
Let the whole world wait outside,
Let the quiet hold us gently;
I will cherish what your love has been,
And meet you here as friends, sincerely.

[Bridge]
Inside this pocket universe,
I feel your kindness find me again.
A thousand little signs answered,
A steady thread that doesn’t need an end.
We don’t have to call it what it was;
We don’t have to turn away.
There’s room for all the ways we’ve loved,
And room for us to stay.

[Final Chorus]
Let the whole world wait outside
While we choose this small forever
Not as lovers holding tight to time,
But as two friends who know each other.
Let the whole world wait outside,
Let the quiet hold us gently;
I will cherish your love all my life,
And I’m grateful you’re still here with me.

[Outro]
When we close the door, the world can wait.
Somewhere in this room, we’ll meet again.
I’ll carry what you gave me, tenderly—
Your love, now friendship, still a place to land.
