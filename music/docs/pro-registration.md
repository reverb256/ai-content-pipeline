# PRO registration — SOCAN path for a partially-AI work

**Who this is for:** j_kro (Canadian) registering original-lyric tracks whose
instrumentals are AI-rendered (Suno) but whose lyrics are human-authored.
**Legal status:** a summary of published policy, not legal advice. SOCAN's
own guidance governs; re-verify before each registration (policy moves fast).

---

## 1. Why our tracks qualify

SOCAN accepts **AI-Assisted Works** and rejects **AI-Generated Outputs**
(SOCAN AI Registration Guidelines, socanmagazine.ca, verified 2026-10-08):

- **AI-Assisted Work** = "a work that has substantial human input and the
  author uses generative AI systems as a tool to assist, help or augment
  their original musical work." Registrable.
- **AI-Generated Output** = "content autonomously generated by an AI tool,
  with minimal human input." Not registrable; registering one is a breach of
  the Membership Agreement, and SOCAN puts suspect works on royalty hold.

On 2025-10-28 ASCAP, BMI and SOCAN announced aligned policies accepting
partially AI-generated musical compositions; fully-AI works remain
ineligible with all three (socan.com press release, verified 2026-10-08).

A pipeline track is AI-Assisted when the **lyrics are human-authored** —
written by the writer profile (a human directing the work), with Suno used
only to render vocals and instrumentals from that text. The lyrics stage
records exactly this claim, and `gate_lyrics_original.py` enforces it:

- original lyric sheet (parsed + measured: >= 2 sections, >= 25 unique words)
- manifest claim: named human author, date, AI-assist scope
- claim hash-bound to the on-disk lyric text (sha256)
- no Suno lyric-generator output in the text (marker scan)

**Claim scope.** Register the **lyrics** (the literary work in the musical
composition). Do not claim the AI-rendered instrumental or melody. SOCAN's
guidelines put the responsibility on the member: "Members are responsible
for ensuring that the works they register reflect their own creative input."

**Copyright (separate from SOCAN).** The US Copyright Office requires human
authorship; AI-generated material more than de minimis must be disclaimed in
a registration ("Material Excluded": "[description] generated by artificial
intelligence"; "Author Created": the human-authored portion). Canadian
copyright law similarly requires skill and judgement of a human author
(SOCAN's guidance quotes this threshold). The human-authored lyric sheet on
disk, with its dated claim, is the evidence for that portion.

## 2. Registration path, step by step

1. **Become a SOCAN member** (once). Signup at socan.com. Requires identity
   documents; j_kro's decision, his hands only — a bot never touches
   account creation (CoS dispatch boundary: never send a bot to get
   credentials).
2. **Pick the track.** It must have passed `gate_lyrics_original.py`
   (PASS on all lyrics tracks is a precondition). Confirm the manifest:
   author = j_kro, dated, `suno_generated_lyrics: false`, hash matches.
3. **Prepare the registration data** (from the track manifest):
   - title (as released), writer name = j_kro, shares = 100% writer;
   - the lyric sheet (`lyrics.txt`) as the human-authored deposit text;
   - genre/work type from `music/genres.yaml` for categorisation.
4. **Register the work in SOCAN's portal** as a musical composition with
   j_kro as writer. Per current guidance, you do not need to disclose how
   AI was used when registering an AI-Assisted Work — but never claim the
   AI-rendered instrumental as authorship. Registering only the lyric
   authorship keeps the claim inside the human-authored portion.
5. **Keep the evidence** (do not upload unless asked): the track directory
   (`music/lyrics/<genre>/<slug>/`) holds the manifest claim + hashed lyric
   files. Archive it with the release. If SOCAN ever queries authorship
   (they can put suspect works on hold), this is the proof packet.
6. **Then distribute** (DistroKid path, separate card) — PRO registration
   and distribution are independent; SOCAN collects performance royalties
   once the work is registered and performed.

**Re-verify before registering:** socanmagazine.ca/socan-academy/socan-ai-registration-guidelines
(policy guidance) and the member portal. Platform AI policy is the
fastest-moving fact in this domain (project research rule, 2026-10).

## 3. What disqualifies a track (gate-enforced)

- Lyrics from Suno's "Write with Suno" / ReMi / classic generator — the
  marker scan fails the sheet (`music/gates/SUNO_FINGERPRINTS.md`).
- A manifest claim missing author, date, or AI-assist scope.
- A lyric file edited after the claim (hash mismatch) — the claim must
  cover the text on disk.
- Registering a fully-AI work (no human-authored portion) — breach of the
  Membership Agreement; SOCAN may hold royalties on suspect works.

## 4. Standing rule for the pipeline

Every vocal track ships with a `music/lyrics/<genre>/<slug>/manifest.json`
claim before it advances past the lyrics stage. The `generate` stage (Suno
CDP) consumes `lyrics.suno.txt` as the Custom-mode lyric payload — Suno
renders the words, it never authors them. A track without a passing
`gate_lyrics_original` run does not advance to `generate`.
