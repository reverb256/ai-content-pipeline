# Reflection — VRChat ERP Intimacy

## Concept
Intimacy. Sheer attention. Hyper focus because you're hermetically sealed in together. How we gently and playfully explore the depths of each others' minds together.

## Mood
Intimate, atmospheric, playful but deep. The feeling of being completely present with someone in a virtual space — where the physical world falls away and it's just two minds exploring each other.
