Rules:
- Slower harmonic rhythm than any other gamer template. Earth does not chase.
- The drone is the ground; instruments are what walk on it. Never bury the drone.
- Loop-safe ending into the intro's drone.

## More Options

- **Weirdness:** 35%. The palette is ancient and analog; novelty must not modernize
  it.
- **Style Influence:** 80%. "Didgeridoo + tribal drums + open fifths" must hold or
  the element blurs into generic world music.
- **Exclude Styles:** ` EDM, synths with fast arpeggios, pop vocals, bright major key, drum machine, trap`
- Drum machines specifically: the heartbeat must sound like hide and wood, not a
  909.

## Craft notes — what makes it read as an EARTH theme

1. **Drone on open fifths = the monolith.** Without a third, major/minor stays
   undecided — "rooted, yet open-ended." That unresolved horizon IS the earth
   element's harmonic signature (the elemental-chime tradition tunes Earth to open
   chords for exactly this reason).
2. **Didgeridoo is the only iconic "earth" instrument.** Its drone breath is both
   ground and breath; nothing else says "ancient land" in one timbre.
3. **Heartbeat pulse.** Deep regular drum (djembe/buffalo frame drum) at slow BPM
   reads as tectonic patience. Earth's tempo is 80–90 — below fire (150), above
   water stillness (82 with no pulse).
4. **Organic materials only:** hide drums, wooden marimba, bone/wood flutes, clap
   sticks, shakers. "Music made from the earth" — leather, wood, breath, stone.
5. **Cave as instrument.** Long reverb + distant echo placement makes the space
   itself audible. Dry mix = warehouse, not cavern.
6. **Ritual dynamics.** The breakdown is not a drop — it is a hush: the feeling of
   standing where something old can hear you.
7. **Loop discipline:** stems + loop_point + seamless_loop, outro into intro drone.

A generation with electronic drums, bright tonality, or rushing tempo is OUT of
genre — reject it.
