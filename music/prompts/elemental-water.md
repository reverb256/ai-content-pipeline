Rules:
- Sad and uplifting at once is the emotional target (Wise's own description of the
  benchmark). Not ominous, not cheerful.
- Timbre morphing is the genre signature: the same arpeggio changing waveforms as it
  runs, like light through water.
- Loop-safe outro into the intro texture.

## More Options

- **Weirdness:** 55%. Water benefits from strangeness — evolving textures, bell-like
  hybrids. But the melody must stay findable.
- **Style Influence:** 70%. "Morphing arps + minor add9 + slow" must hold; the
  evolution is where freshness lives.
- **Exclude Styles:** ` drums kit, aggressive percussion, EDM, vocals, major key pop, horror drone`
- Water is flow, not fear: a horror-leaning generation is wrong-element.

## Craft notes — what makes it read as a WATER theme

1. **Wave-sequencing / evolving arpeggios are the genre's core sound.** Aquatic
   Ambience was born from a Korg Wavestation preset ("The Wave Song"): eight samples
   cycling in sequence so the bass and arp continuously EVOLVE. A static arp is a
   pad; a morphing arp is water.
2. **Minor key with add9/ninth harmony** is the benchmark's harmonic DNA
   (Cmadd9 – Abadd9 – F7sus4 – Bbadd9). Melancholy + air = the "sad and uplifting
   at once" feel.
3. **Pitch-bent whale-call tones** (Shepard-style illusions that seem to rise
   forever) are the signature melodic gesture of underwater scoring.
4. **Percussion as sonar, not drums.** Morphing pings, filtered ticks — rhythm that
   reads as submarine distance, never a kit.
5. **Tempo 80–90 and slower harmonic rhythm.** Water does not rush. Chord changes
   every 4+ bars; motion lives in the texture, not the harmony.
6. **Cavernous reverb** is the space itself. The mix should feel like the listener
   is suspended in a medium — distant, blurred, gentle.
7. **Loop discipline:** same as all gamer lanes — stems + loop_point + seamless_loop.
   The outro resolves into the intro's texture.

A generation with a drum kit, major-key bounce, or menace is OUT of genre — reject it.
