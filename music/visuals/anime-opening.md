# visual: anime-opening (anime-opening)

> Pairs with: `prompts/anime-opening.md`. Video shape: single-4min
> showpiece. The opening's visual must look like the first 90 seconds
> of a TV opening: a character running toward something, sky, speed.

## Palette & scene

- Sky gradient: pre-dawn indigo #1a1b4b → dawn gold #ffd76e →
  horizon coral #ff7a5c, with the last chorus bursting into full
  daylight #fff3d6.
- Scene: a lone figure (back view, avatar-style) running across a
  rooftop / bridge / cloud layer toward the light. Scale is huge —
  the horizon line sits low, the sky takes 70% of frame.
- Speed lines and cloud strata as the motion environment.

## Motion grammar

- The camera RUNS with the figure: constant forward push (2–4 px/frame),
  never static. Parallax layers: far clouds slow, near rooftops fast.
- Verse = mid-shot drift with wind in the figure's hair/coat.
- Pre-chorus = camera tilts up, clouds accelerate (build).
- Chorus = full flare: speed lines radiate from center, bloom on the
  gold, 2-frame strobe on drum accents.
- **Final chorus key change = the sky bursts to daylight.** The visual
  key change is the light itself — this mirrors the audio's signature
  move and is the shot the whole video is built to earn.
- Outro = hard cut to a title card frame on the final unison hit.
  No fade.

## Texture

- Cel-shaded line work, 2% chromatic aberration on chorus frames
  only. Light particle scatter (pollen/embers) tied to drum fills.
- Typography: the track title in a Japanese-style vertical title
  card at the final hit, chapter-safe.

## Loop & variation (gate_youtube_authentic)

- Each song gets its own figure, horizon geography, and light color.
  No reuse of the sky gradient across songs — the key-change daylight
  burst must be a fresh composition each time.

## Audio-visual sync

- Double-kick fills = particle bursts. Brass stabs = full-frame bloom
  flash. The bridge's half-time = the running figure slows, camera
  holds. The final-chorus key change = daylight burst + vertical
  camera rise. The ending unison hit = title card, hard cut.
