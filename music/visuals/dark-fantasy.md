# visual: dark-fantasy (epic-orchestral-boss)

> Pairs with: `prompts/epic-orchestral-boss.md`. Video shape: single-4min.

## Palette & scene

- Obsidian dark #0e0b12, blood crimson #8c1f28, cold steel #6e7b8a, ethereal
  pale gold #d8c9a3 for the choir/magic accents.
- Scene: the boss arena. Gargantuan scale, backlit silhouette of the boss, one
  light source (throne fire / rift / eclipse). This is a SHOWPIECE video: the
  scene must be composed like a film frame, not a loop wallpaper.

## Motion grammar

- Three-act camera mirrors the music: Act I dread = slow push-in on darkness
  (0.5 px/frame). Act II escalation = orbiting mid-shots, impacts shake 2–4 px.
  Breakdown silence = hold on black with a single ember. Climax = rapid cuts ON
  the choir hits, maximum bloom, 2–4 frame strobes on downbeats.
- Final hit = hard cut to black. No fade.

## Texture

- Volumetric haze, ember particles rising, chromatic aberration 2% on impact
  frames only. Film grain 5%.

## Loop & variation (gate_youtube_authentic)

- Single-4min showpiece: each song gets its own boss silhouette and arena. No
  reuse of arena frames across songs.

## Audio-visual sync

- Sub-boom impacts = full-frame flash + shake. String ostinato = ember rise rate
  (the visual engine matches the audio engine). The false-ending silence = pure
  black, one ember — the breath before the wall of sound.
