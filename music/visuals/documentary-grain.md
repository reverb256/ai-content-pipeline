# visual: documentary-grain (activist)

> Pairs with: `prompts/activist.md`. Video shape: lyric-video.

## Palette & scene

- Handheld documentary truth: desaturated earth tones, #6b6257 (dust), #9c8f7a
  (skin/paper), #232019 (shadow). One accent color per video — the movement's
  color (a protest sign, a banner) — used sparingly.

## Motion grammar

- 16 mm film look: handheld sway, gate weave, occasional focus hunt. Deliberate
  imperfection = credibility. Steadicam polish reads as an ad, and this lane
  must not look like an ad.
- Lyric video: hand-lettered or typewriter lyrics appear ON the phrase, not after
  it. Lines assemble over archival-style textures (paper, concrete, protest wood).

## Texture

- Grain 6–8%, slight halation on highlights, 4:3 or 16:9 letterboxed archival
  inserts. No glossy kinetic-type effects.

## Loop & variation (gate_youtube_authentic)

- Each song's video is its own artifact — no template reuse across songs beyond the
  palette rules. Vary the medium (super-8 vs newsprint collage vs chalk on
  pavement).

## Audio-visual sync

- Group vocals in the chorus: lyrics swap to a crowd of hands/voices motif — many
  markers writing the same line. The visual argument of the genre: one voice
  becomes many.

## Ad-suitability

- Expect limited monetization. Do not design thumbnails for clickbait; design for
  the documentary supervisor who might license the track.
