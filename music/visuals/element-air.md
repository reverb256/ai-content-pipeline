# visual: element-air (elemental-air)

> Pairs with: `prompts/elemental-air.md`. Video shape: loop-10min.

## Palette & scene

- Open-sky bright: cloud white #f4f7fa, high-altitude blue #9fc4e8, pale gold
  sun-veil #e8d9b0. NO ground horizon — pure altitude.
- Scene: above-cloud drift / cirrus fields / mountain-top mist. Always the
  feeling of height without vertigo edges.

## Motion grammar

- Cloud parallax at 3 layers (near/mid/far), speeds 2:1.5:0.5 px/s — gentle
  forward glide, never a rush.
- Chime sparkles: single-frame iridescent glints at random positions above the
  cloud line, sparse (1–3 per 8 s).

## Texture

- Soft light bloom, film grain 2% (the lightest of all visuals), airy haze
  between layers.

## Loop & variation (gate_youtube_authentic)

- Rotate dawn / noon / high-wind cloudscape scenes per upload.

## Audio-visual sync

- Flute phrases = light breeze crossing (cloud edge sharpens slightly on the
  breath attack). Chime tones = the glints. The near-silence breakdown = full
  whiteout mist for one phrase, clearing as the flutes return.
