# visual: element-fire (elemental-fire)

> Pairs with: `prompts/elemental-fire.md`. Video shape: loop-10min.

## Palette & scene

- Charcoal bed #1a1512, ember core #ff6a1a, molten highlights #ffd24a, cool rim
  #2e475d for contrast (hot reads hotter with a cold rim).
- Scene: coal-bed macro / forge coals / lava crack field. NO cartoon flame sprites.

## Motion grammar

- Ember flicker at 12–24 Hz organic jitter; heat-haze shimmer above the brightest
  zones (2–4 px displacement, 0.5–1 Hz).
- On the smoldering breakdown: coals dim to 30%, smoke drift replaces flame.
- Loop-10min: one period for the whole video — the delivered loop's own length
  (`music/genres.yaml` `loop_bars`: 25 bars = 40.000 s at 150 BPM, tiling exactly 15x into
  the 600.000 s shape; card t_b75ccb4a). The visual cycle must equal that period, not an
  8-bar cycle: 600 / 12.8 = 46.875 is fractional, and a visual period that differs from the
  audio's re-phases the arc (the reprise flash would land elsewhere every cycle). Within the
  period the ember positions reset exactly, so the cycle tiles seamlessly.

## Texture

- Black-crackle grain, 6% dust, bloom only on ember cores.

## Loop & variation (gate_youtube_authentic)

- Rotate coal-field / forge / volcano-crack scenes per upload. Palette constant.

## Audio-visual sync

- Taiko hits = ember flare pulses (per hit, ±10% luminance). String ostinato =
  shimmer speed (visual shimmer tracks ostinato tempo). Peak = brief white-hot
  flash ON the downbeat of the reprise.
