# visual: element-water (elemental-water)

> Pairs with: `prompts/elemental-water.md`. Video shape: loop-10min.

## Palette & scene

- Deep teal #0b2e33, mid cyan #1e6f78, caustic light #cdeef2. Bioluminescent dot
  accents #6ff2c8, sparse.
- Scene: sunlit shallows / abyssal drift / cavern lake. Light rays from above
  (god rays), particles suspended.

## Motion grammar

- Everything at drift speed: rays rotate 0.5°/s, particles 1–3 px/s, kelp/sway
  6–10 s period. NOTHING moves faster than bubbles rise.
- Caustic light webs on rock surfaces, 0.5–1 Hz morph.

## Texture

- Water blur on distant layers, 3% marine snow, vignette to darkness at edges —
  depth IS the composition.

## Loop & variation (gate_youtube_authentic)

- Rotate shallows / cavern / abyss scenes per upload. Same palette, different
  depth.

## Audio-visual sync

- Arp cycles = caustic shimmer phase. Whale-call tones = a distant large shape
  passing through the rays (once per phrase). Breakdown = light dims to abyssal
  blue-green, then slowly returns.
