# visual: lofi-room-rain (chill-lounge)

> Pairs with: `prompts/chill-lounge.md`. Video shape: album-30-60min.

## Palette & scene

- Warm dark interior: amber desk lamp, muted teal window, rain-streaked glass.
- Base tones #2a2321, #d9a066 (lamp), #4a6670 (window). Never saturated pastels.
- One continuous scene per video; weather/time-of-day varies between uploads.

## Motion grammar

- 0.5–2 px/frame drift (slow pan or parallax). Nothing faster.
- Rain streaks on glass at ~12 fps; steam/boil cycles 8–12 s; vinyl turntable at
  33 rpm exact.
- Cut only on track boundaries. Within a track: no cuts.

## Texture

- Film grain 4–6%, gentle vignette, 8–12 kHz-soft ambience visuals (dust motes).
- The room must look lived-in: books, mug, cable mess. Empty rooms read as AI slop.

## Loop & variation (gate_youtube_authentic)

- Build each album video from a DIFFERENT room variant (rain/coffee shop/night
  porch/studio). Vary lamp position, window view, season. Never reuse the exact
  frame stack across uploads.
- 30–60 min runtime = 8–12 tracks + chapter cards between, not one static image.

## Audio-visual sync

- Track changes announce with a slow 2 s crossfade; lamp flicker optional on
  downbeats only.
