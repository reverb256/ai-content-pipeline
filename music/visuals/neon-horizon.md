# visual: neon-horizon (trance)

> Pairs with: `prompts/trance.md`. Video shape: mix-60min.

## Palette & scene

- Night-to-dawn gradient horizon: deep indigo #14163a → electric cyan #35e0ff →
  magenta sunline #ff4fa3. City/mountain silhouette layer.
- Wireframe grid floor, optional. Scanlines off — that is a different genre.

## Motion grammar

- The sunline PULSES with the 138 BPM four-on-the-floor (±2% brightness per kick).
- Build sections: horizon rises 1–2 px/bar. Breakdown: grid fades, star field
  breathes. Drop: full flare + radial speed lines.
- Camera drifts forward at constant velocity; never wobbles.

## Texture

- VHS chroma bleed 3–5% on the magenta only. Light bloom on all light sources.
- Tracklist typography bottom-left, chapter-safe.

## Loop & variation (gate_youtube_authentic)

- Each mix video uses a different horizon geography (coastline/range/city/desert).
- 60 min = DJ-mix format: continuous visual, no per-track cuts — one journey
  through dawn.

## Audio-visual sync

- Kick = horizon pulse. Snare-roll build = accelerating star streaks. Two-beat
  silence before drop = horizon holds its breath (full 2 s freeze).
