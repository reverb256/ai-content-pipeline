# visual: soft-window-light (sentimental-piano)

> Pairs with: `prompts/sentimental-piano.md`. Video shape: album-30-60min.

## Palette & scene

- One window, one instrument, one hour of light. Cream curtain, warm sidelight,
  dust motes. Palette: #f2e8d5 (light), #8a7a66 (wood), #3a342c (shadow).
- The piano is felt-era intimate: slightly worn, sheet music, half-full tea cup.

## Motion grammar

- Light does the moving, nothing else: curtain sway (8 s period), dust motes
  (Brownian drift), cloud shadows crossing the floor every 40–90 s.
- Camera fixed or imperceptibly slow dolly. No cuts within a track.

## Texture

- Soft-focus lens, 3% grain, gentle bloom on the window highlights.
- "Memory" grade: lifted blacks, faded 2–5%, never crisp digital.

## Loop & variation (gate_youtube_authentic)

- Vary the light story per upload: morning rain / golden hour / winter blue / dusk
  lamp. Same room layout is fine; light + season must differ.
- 30–60 min album format: chapter fades between tracks.

## Audio-visual sync

- The reversed swells before phrases = light briefly warming (subtle, 1 s).
- Emotional bloom: cloud clears — the brightest moment lands with the music's peak.
