# visual: urban-grain (jungle-dnb)

> Pairs with: `prompts/jungle-dnb.md`. Video shape: mix-60min.

## Palette & scene

- 1994 London night: sodium streetlight orange #e8871e, wet asphalt black #101216,
  flickering shopfront neon, tower-block silhouettes.
- Pirate-radio props: transmission tower, speaker stacks, sound-system poster wall.

## Motion grammar

- Frame-rate play is the signature: chop between 12/24 fps every few bars, like a
  chopped break. Stutter-cuts (2–4 frames) ON the chopped snares.
- Rewind moments (airhorn hits): 0.5 s vertical roll + tape rewinder overlay.

## Texture

- 12-bit sampler grit: heavy grain 8–10%, slight posterize, occasional VHS head
  switching noise bar at the bottom.
- Illegal-pirate-radio energy, not clean festival visuals.

## Loop & variation (gate_youtube_authentic)

- Rotate street corner / rooftop / warehouse doorway per upload. Same palette,
  different geography.
- 60 min continuous mix format — cut visual scenes on phrase boundaries (every 32
  bars max).

## Audio-visual sync

- Sub-bass slides = slow zoom pulses. Ghost snares = single-frame flash edits.
  Airhorn = the rewind roll.
