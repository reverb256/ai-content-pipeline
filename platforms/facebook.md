# Facebook Platform Playbook

> Facebook is the reach + community surface. Less technical than X/LinkedIn,
> but the widest base of non-technical audience and the strongest group/community
> mechanics. Primary use: cross-posting the flagship + audio drama distribution.

## Platform Rules

- 1-2 posts/week on the Page (not personal profile)
- Cross-post from flagship with Facebook framing (story-first, not tech-first)
- Video/audio posts get the widest reach — audio dramas should land here as
  native uploads (or link + thumbnail if native upload is unavailable)
- Groups > Page for engagement: niche groups (Winnipeg tech, AI hobbyists,
  Canadian devs) where allowed
- Respond to comments in the first 2 hours
- No hashtags (Facebook punishes them); 1-2 keywords max

## What Fits Facebook

- Audio drama episode drops (native video/audio, thumbnail + hook caption)
- Behind-the-scenes of the content machine (human interest)
- Build-in-public updates in plain language (not technical)
- Community questions ("what should we build next") — good group fodder

## Interaction Mode

Facebook interacts via the CDP browser (real reverb256 session) or the
Meta Business Suite API. Page access requires the Page's access token or a
Browser session logged into the Page.
