# LinkedIn Platform Playbook

> LinkedIn is the professional/B2B surface. Secondary for now — the
> engineering audience is thinner here, but the operator/decision-maker
> audience is present.

## Platform Rules

- 2 posts/week (professional cadence, not daily)
- Lead with the operator lesson, internal decision, or before/after workflow
- Long-form posts + carousels work here
- Comment thoughtfully on relevant content (visibility + relationships)
- Cross-post from the flagship piece with LinkedIn framing, not the same text

## What Fits LinkedIn (from our pillars)

- "The migration decision framework" (B2B operators)
- "How we run a 6-bot content team" (organizational angle)
- "What the Omarchy migration taught us about lock-in" (thought leadership)
- Carousels: the loop diagram, the role contract table, the playbook structure

## What Does NOT Fit

- Technical code dumps without context
- Raw build logs
- Content that only NixOS insiders understand (translate the lesson)

## Interaction Mode

LinkedIn interacts via the CDP browser (real reverb256 session). Feed reads,
posting, analytics — via the dashboard or captured API.

## Metrics

- Profile views, post impressions, reactions
- Comments (quality over quantity)
- Connection growth in target segments (founders, operators, AI builders)
